## 💜 Hi there!

I'm Julia, a computer science student with a particular interest in cybersecurity and artificial intelligence.

I've always been curious about how technology works, but the more I learn, the more I realize just how much there is to discover. So, instead of keeping my notes scattered across notebooks, random files, and approximately a million browser tabs, I decided to put everything in one place.

This repository is where I collect what I'm learning, experiment with new concepts, write code, break things (occasionally on purpose), and hopefully get a little better every day.

You'll find everything here, from binary numbers and Python basics to networking, operating systems, cybersecurity, and eventually more advanced AI projects.

Some things might be very basic. Others might be a little more ambitious. That's kind of the point — I want to document the whole process, not just the finished results.

---

## 🪻 A Little About Me

I already hold a **Bachelor's degree in Digital Communication**, and I'm currently studying **Computer Science**, specializing in computer networks and information systems security.

My background in Digital Communication has given me a different perspective on how technology affects people and society, while my computer science studies allow me to explore the technical side of it all.

My main interests are cybersecurity, especially offensive security and penetration testing, and artificial intelligence. Eventually, I'd love to combine both fields and specialize in AI security.

Outside of computers, I'm a huge cat person, an enthusiastic collector of books, and someone who genuinely enjoys learning new things.

## 🎯 What I'm Working Towards

Right now, my priority is building a solid understanding of computer science rather than rushing through topics just to say I've completed them.

I'm particularly focused on improving my Python skills, becoming more comfortable with Linux, strengthening my networking knowledge, and getting more hands-on experience with cybersecurity.

I'm also exploring computer vision and YOLO as part of my engineering thesis.

Long-term, I'd love to work in offensive security and eventually specialize in AI security. There's something fascinating about understanding not only how intelligent systems work, but also how they can fail, be manipulated, and be made more secure.

## 🧪 How I Learn

My approach is pretty simple:

1. Understand the theory.
2. Try it myself.
3. Break something.
4. Figure out why it broke.
5. Fix it.
6. Document what I learned.

And then probably find another five things I want to learn along the way.

This repository isn't supposed to be perfect. It's supposed to show progress, curiosity, and the work behind developing real technical skills.
